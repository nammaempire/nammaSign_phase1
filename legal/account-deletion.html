Delete your account & data

App: Reset95 (com.nammaempire.nammasign) · Last updated: 10 August 2026

You can delete your Reset95 account and personal data at any time. There are two ways to do it — directly in the app, or by sending us a request. Both remove the same data.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Reset95 app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and associated data are deleted right away.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can’t access the app, email us and we’ll delete your account for you:

Send an email to: reset95@gmail.com
From: the email address (or mentioning the mobile number) registered on your account
Subject: “Delete my Reset95 account”

We verify that the request comes from the account holder, then process it. We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • your profile (name, email, phone number, and business/KYC details);
  • your uploaded KYC documents and profile image;
  • your advertising creatives (images/videos) stored for your bookings;
  • your notifications and app preferences; and
  • your sign-in credentials (your authentication record).

What we may retain (and why)

RetainedReasonPeriod
Invoices and transaction/payment records (amount, tax, payment identifiers) Legal, tax, and accounting obligations under Indian law Up to 8 years (statutory), then deleted

Retained records are kept secure and are not used for any other purpose. Where the law allows, we de-identify records rather than keep them tied to you.

Questions

If you need help with deletion or have questions about your data, contact reset95@gmail.com. For full details of how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
