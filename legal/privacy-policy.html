Reset95 (“Reset95”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is an advertising marketplace that lets you book advertising slots on LED billboards in Bengaluru, India. This policy explains what personal data we collect through the Reset95 mobile app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Reset95 is operated by [Registered legal entity name], [registered business address, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India] (“Namma Empire”). For any privacy question, contact us at reset95@gmail.com.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to everyone who uses the Reset95 Android application and the services offered through it — both individual advertisers and business (corporate) advertisers. By creating an account and using Reset95, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the marketplace, verify advertisers, place ads, and take payment.

2.1 Information you provide

DataWhy we collect it

2.2 Payment information

Payments are processed by our payment gateway, Razorpay Software Private Limited. When you pay, you enter your payment details (UPI ID, card, or netbanking) directly into Razorpay’s secure checkout. Reset95 does not receive or store your full card number, UPI PIN, or banking credentials. We store only a record of the transaction — the amount, the payment method type, the Razorpay order and payment identifiers, and whether the payment succeeded — so we can confirm your booking and provide an invoice.

2.3 Information collected automatically

DataWhy we collect it

App activity & analytics — screens viewed, booking funnel events, and similar in-app interactionsTo understand how the app is used and improve it (via Google Firebase Analytics).
Crash & diagnostic data — crash logs, device model, OS version, and error diagnosticsTo detect and fix stability problems (via Google Firebase Crashlytics).
App-integrity signalsTo protect against fraud and abuse of our backend (via Firebase App Check / Google Play Integrity).

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • create and secure your account and sign you in;
  • verify individual and business advertisers (KYC) and comply with tax and legal obligations;
  • process bookings, display your advertisements, and take and confirm payment;
  • send you service messages and booking status updates;
  • review advertising content for compliance with our content rules before it goes live;
  • provide invoices and customer support;
  • detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues; and
  • maintain, analyse, and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure. Our primary region is configured in India (asia-south1). Google may process certain operational data (such as crash and analytics data) on servers outside India under Google’s own safeguards.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and booking data for as long as your account is active and as long as needed to provide the service. After you delete your account (see Section 8), we delete or de-identify your personal data, except where we must retain certain records — for example, invoices and transaction records — to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations under Indian law (commonly up to 8 years for financial records).

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our backend is controlled by Firebase Security Rules and server-side checks, and payment credentials are handled by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS-compliant provider. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit access to it.

8. Your choices and rights

Access, correction, and deletion

You can view and edit much of your profile inside the app. You can delete your account and associated data at any time from Profile → Delete account inside the app, or by emailing reset95@gmail.com from your registered address. Full details, including what is deleted and what is retained, are on our Account & Data Deletion page.

Notifications

You can turn off push notifications in your device settings at any time.

9. Children

Reset95 is a business/advertising service intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
