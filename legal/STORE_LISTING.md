## Content rating (IARC questionnaire notes)

Reset95 has no violence, sexual, or objectionable content and is a business/
utility app. Answer the questionnaire honestly — expected outcome is
**“Everyone” / Rated for 3+** (or PEGI 3). Notes for the questionnaire:

- Category: **Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other** (business tool).
- No user-to-user free communication; no user-generated content shown to other
  users (creatives are reviewed and displayed on billboards, not shared in-app).
- Digital purchases: **Yes** — users pay for advertising slots in-app.
- No violence, sexuality, profanity, drugs, or gambling.

Even though the rating may be “Everyone,” keep your **target audience 18+** in
App content → Target audience, because it's a paid business/advertising service.

## Graphic assets checklist (you still need to create these)

- **App icon** — 512 × 512 px, 32-bit PNG (you already have the Reset95 “R” mark;
  export at 512²).

  the listing.
- **Phone screenshots** — at least **2** (up to 8), 16:9 or 9:16, min 320 px,
  max 3840 px. Good picks: the board list, the booking/preview screen, the
  Review & Pay screen, and the success screen.

  (YouTube URL).
